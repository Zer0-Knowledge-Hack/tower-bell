01 · THE PROBLEM

To know what is here
you need the internet.

Maps. Instagram. The shop’s Wi‑Fi. If the signal dies, the street goes blank.

02 · THE PROBLEM

To know what is here
you need an account.

Someone else’s app. Someone else’s login. Your walk becomes their funnel.

03 · THE PROBLEM

To know what is here
you need luck.

Is it open? Is the promo still on? You guess, or you walk past.

04 · THE IDEA

Phone to phone.
No cloud in the middle.

Towerbell finds shops around you in seconds. Direct. Private. Built for the sidewalk, not a datacenter.

05 · HOW IT WORKS

1. FIND Devices meet on the street. Same topic. No server matching them.
2. TELL The shop sends a tiny record: name, category, hours, promo.
3. SEE The traveler sees it now. Offline. No account.

06 · WHY IT MATTERS

NO SIGNAL You are blind. The block might as well be empty.
WITH APPS You connect when there is coverage — and only if you have the right login.
TOWERBELL Phones talk to phones. You discover what is actually next to you.

07 · WHAT YOU INSTALL

A tool, not a website.

SCAN listens for nearby beacons. BEACON broadcasts the shop.

Judges install a standalone CLI (TUI). The 8-bit phone UI is the same idea on a screen.
